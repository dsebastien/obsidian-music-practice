## The session note carries the practice time (request from the vault's activity-time change)

- The vault moved activity time to session notes (notesSeb commits 2a3f40fa04, 354315f947, 0fe266351f; rule in `.claude/skills/osk-learning-shared/references/learning-rules.md`, "Activity time"; Kanban Action Planner 2.9.0 "Time from linked notes", its Business Rule 78). A practice log here still wrote one time entry per log on the activity and added the minutes to its duration, so the activity grew again and the stored duration drifted from the recomputed one.
- Now a log writes no time entry and adds nothing. After the session note, it waits (10 s at most) until `metadataCache.resolvedLinks` has the session → activity link, then recomputes the activity's duration and last-session fields in one `processFrontMatter` (entries read again there): `domain/activity-time.ts` (`planActivityTime`, `applyActivityTime`, `linkedSessionOf`), the linked sessions from the link index (`linkedSessions`). Results: `updated`, `unchanged`, `missing`, `not-linked`, `skipped`; `addTimeEntry` and its `added` / `exists` / `adopted` results are gone.
- Why a pure function in the plugin and not the Kanban Action Planner's `api.computeTrackedTime`: (1) KAP counts linked notes only when "Time from linked notes" is on for the activity's type; with it off, its total leaves the session out and a write would drop the log's minutes; (2) its index updates on metadata events, so the log would wait on KAP's index as well as Obsidian's, with no way to tell "not indexed yet" from "not counted"; (3) the rule is the same code shape as the vault's `learning.ts activity-time` (the other writers), it works without KAP, and it is unit-tested. Cost: the rule lives in two places (here and KAP), kept equal by the specs.
- Adopted timer: the timer is stopped, its own entry (same start within a minute) is removed, then the activity is recomputed. If the entry doesn't appear within 10 s, the activity is recomputed anyway (`missing`) and the notice says to delete the entry if it appears, as `learning.ts log` does.
- A session type without the activities field can't link, so the log doesn't wait: the activity is left alone with a warning. `not-linked` warns too, pointing to KAP's Recompute tracked time.
- Undo: unchanged mechanics. The activity keys the log changed (the entries list with the timer's entry, duration, last session) go back to their values from before the log, so the timer's entry comes back; no recompute after the undo (it would have to wait for the index to drop the trashed session, and the keys already hold the pre-log state). Notices updated.
- Settings: the Time entries field stays (read for the recompute, used for the adopted timer); its description says so.
- Specs: `activity-time.spec.ts` (links, tags, minutes, the recompute, skipped session entries, the adopted entry, rounding and entry days, nothing to count, non-list entries, key case), `practice-log.service.spec.ts` (recompute through a fake app, adopted timer removal and its undo, non-list entries). 418 tests.
- Checked against the plugin main's local music-notation build (the published 0.8.0 lacks APIs main uses; copied into this worktree's node_modules, not committed).

## Metronome count-in, beat flash, the note's time signature (#77), and URI actions (#47)

- #77, count-in: `metronome.countInBars` (0–4, default 1) clicks one bar of beats before the first bar, one click a beat whatever the clicks setting (the first in its own 2000 Hz pitch). The first bar starts a beat after the last count-in click, and the speed trainer counts bars only from there. The beat dots go hollow during the count-in, and the metronome shows "Count-in…". A chord change drill counts 3, 2, 1 seconds before its time when the count-in is on (`drillCountIn`); changes aren't counted meanwhile. Settings → Metronome and the metronome itself have the control.
- #77, flash: `metronome.flash` (default off) shows a light under the beat dots that turns on for 120 ms at each beat: accent color on beats, the error color on beat one, muted during the count-in; no transition with reduced motion. It follows `state.pulse`, a beat counter the scheduler moves when a beat sounds (never on a subdivision), so the same beat twice in a row still flashes.
- #77, time signature: new field `timeSignature` (`time_signature`, the music-works type's property). `meterOf` reads n/2, n/4, n/8, n/16 (2–12 beats), compound meters as dotted beats of triplets (6/8 → 2 × 3, as the strum blocks count them), `C` and `¢`. The metronome takes it from its item (the command, Speed trainer, the panel's ♩ button) when it isn't running, uses it instead of the settings' beats (and clicks for compound meters), and says "6/8 (2 beats), from the note". Never saved; choosing beats or clicks by hand drops it.
- #47: `obsidian://music-practice?do=start-timer|stop-timer|log|practice-next|plan[&minutes=n]`. The issue suggested `action=`, but Obsidian keeps the handler's name in `action` (`ObsidianProtocolData.action`), so a query `action` can collide with it: the action is `do`, and a query `action` other than `music-practice` is still read. `parseUriRequest` (pure, specs) refuses unknown actions, unknown parameters (besides `vault`), minutes outside 1–1440 or on actions that don't take them, with a notice that says what to write. Only `start-timer` writes without a modal; the others open the modals the commands open (Practice next became `openPracticeNext()`; `openPlan` takes the minutes). The handler waits for the layout, so a URI that starts Obsidian works once the note model loads.
- Specs: `metronome.spec.ts` (meters, count-in beats, defaults), `metronome.service.spec.ts` (new: a fake window and audio clock; count-in clicks and timing, pulses on beats only, no count-in, the trainer after the count-in, the item's meter and when it applies), `uri.spec.ts` (new), `plugin-settings.spec.ts` (new settings fall back). 436 tests.
- Not checked in Obsidian: the clicks and the flash at speed (timing of the light against the sound), the hollow count-in dots, a 6/8 piece opening the metronome in 2 beats of triplets, the drill's 3-2-1, and each URI from outside Obsidian (from a cold start too, and with `vault=`).

## Fix: undo right after a log brought the session note back

Found by the vault test of 0.11: `logPractice` then `undoLastLog` at once left the session note in the vault a second later. Templater's "trigger on file creation" (TPL Dispatcher on `/`) writes every new note again about 1 to 2 s after its creation; a note trashed before that write is created again by it, with its full frontmatter, so the orphan session counted toward the activity at the next recompute.

- `domain/settle.ts`: undo waits until the session note is settled (3 s since its creation, 1 s since its last write, at most 5 s; an older note at once), then runs its checks and trashes it. 2 s was not enough: one vault run of three had Templater's write after it.
- After the trash, undo watches the path for 5 s: a note created again there with the body the log wrote is trashed again, and the recorded keys back as the log left them are put back once more (`loggedKeys` in `domain/undo.ts`). Anything else is left alone. Why the body: Templater's late write leaves the body as the log wrote it, a person's edit doesn't; the frontmatter is stamped by other plugins and is not a sign of a user edit.
- Specs: a fake vault with a virtual clock and a late write at 1 s (undo waits for it) and at 3.5 s (the watch trashes the re-created note); a note created long ago is trashed at once. They failed before the fix.
- Checked in the vault (build installed, plugin reloaded): three runs of log then immediate undo; 6 s later the session note is gone, the activity and the item are byte-identical, no console error; undo took 1.9 to 2.3 s. The practice timer was not touched.
- Business Rules: the URI line said a query `action` other than `music-practice` is read; Obsidian replaces it with the handler's name, so only `do=` carries the action (rule and `uri.ts` comment corrected).

## Fix: a log right after an undo lost its session note

Found by the vault test after the undo race fix: two log and undo cycles back to back left G Major at 32 minutes instead of 30. Trace in the vault: the first undo trashed its note at 3.5 s and watched the path until 8.5 s; the second log created its note at 4.7 s on the same path (the trashed note freed the name) with the same body (same request); the watch took it for a late re-creation and trashed it at 4.9 s, while that log was still writing. The log wrote the item (32), waited 10 s for a link that could not come, then threw ENOENT before it recorded its undo, so its item update could not be undone.

- A new log ends the previous undo's watch at its start (`endUndoWatch`), before any check, so even a refused request ends it.
- The watch only trashes a re-created note. It no longer puts recorded keys back: undo restores them before it returns, and that restore could revert a new log's values when they equal the old ones (`loggedKeys` removed).
- Specs: the fake clock now holds long waits until the spec moves time forward (the 5 s watch had closed at once, so the new specs passed before the fix); a log right after an undo keeps its note; two cycles within the watch trash only their own note; the watch writes no frontmatter. They failed before the fix.
- Checked in the vault: two, then three back-to-back log and undo runs (12 checks each, all pass); a log right after an undo, kept 6 s, then undone; the single-undo race three times (undo 2.9 s). 8 s after each, G Major and Learn Guitar (Activity) are identical to git HEAD, with no session note left and no console error.
