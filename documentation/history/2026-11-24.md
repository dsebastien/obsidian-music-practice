## Playback settings for the room and the amplifier (branch `feat/room-and-amp-settings`)

- Sébastien skipped the second sound check ("skip that"), so the pin was merged (pull request #138), and asked for the settings in the same release as the pin. He chose numbers, like Volume and Strum, over dropdowns of presets: the amplifier's values are his to choose by ear, and presets would fix values nobody heard.
- Four settings in Settings → Playback, after Volume: Room level (0 to 100, 12.5 by default), Room length (seconds, 0.2 to 3, 1.2), Amplifier cabinet (a toggle, off), Amplifier drive (a whole number, 0 to 100, 0). `playEffects` (`domain/playback.ts`) turns them into the library's effects; the Business Rule "The room and the amplifier" has the detail.
- **What "off" sends.** The room at 0 with no amplifier is `dry`: no chain is built, so no room is computed (the way out for a slow device, after the dropout counts of the first check) and there is no ceiling: the plugin's sound of 0.29.0. The room at 0 with an amplifier is a room at 0, because `dry` leaves out the amplifier.
- **Every play reads them**: the library's bars (`playOptions`: `reverb`, `room`, `dry`, `amp` as functions), tabs and scores (`TabHost.effects`), Play this chord or scale and the backing loop (`guitarPlayer`, which `enginePlayer` wraps its player with), the note drill (`effectsNow`).
