## The pinned build in Obsidian: the routes are right, the sound could not be judged (machine too busy)

- Sébastien's vault (Linux, Obsidian 1.15.1, the laptop's built-in output, 48 kHz), a throwaway note with a `chord` block (the sound pack, through the hidden alphaTab player), a `chord` block with `engine: light` and a `tab` block. The released 0.29.0 first, then this branch's build (only `main.js` differs), each block played once to load and once recorded. Measured two ways: the connections each play makes (a log on `AudioNode.prototype.connect`, taken out after) and Chrome's own dropout count (`AudioContext.playbackStats`) inside the app; and after the app, a recording of the output's monitor (`parec`).
- **The routes, by the connections.** Released build: every play goes straight to the speakers (`AudioWorkletNode->speakers` for the sound pack's chord and the tab, `GainNode->speakers` for the light synthesizer). This branch's build: every play goes into the library's chain (convolver, then the wave shaper, then the speakers; `AudioWorkletNode->GainNode` for the two alphaTab routes). alphaTab's audio contexts take a `destination` property of their own in Obsidian's Electron (the first time this route was tried there): the fallback was not needed.
- **After the app.** Nothing is cut flat in any take (0% at or above full scale; the sound pack's chord peaks at 0.863 with the released build and 0.873 with this one). Left and right are the same with the released build (1.0000) and a little apart with this one (0.9987 the chord, 0.9965 the light synthesizer, 0.9996 the tab): the room is there.
- **The sound itself could not be judged.** The machine was very busy with other work during the check (load average 38 to 44 on 12 cores: a local language model, a backup, test suites). The recordings have gaps of silence with both builds (released: 6 gaps in the chord, up to 395 ms, and 25 in the tab; this build: 14 gaps in the light synthesizer's chord, up to 714 ms). So no level or envelope of these takes is worth comparing.
- **To check again on a quiet machine: dropouts with the room on.** Chrome's own count over the same minutes: the released build's three contexts had 2 underruns (21 ms) in about 40 s; this build's three contexts had 94, 78 and 348 (1.0 s, 0.8 s and 3.7 s) in 35 to 53 s.
