# v0.21 Ear training (#72)

Status: built on branch `ear-training`.

## Goal

Ear training inside the vault: hear an interval, a chord, a scale or a scale degree and name it. Four more kinds of the note, chord and scale drills (#73, #91), with their rules: one drill note per kind and setting, the weak-spot map, 20 questions, what you miss asked most, a review on Save.

## Decisions (Sébastien, 2026-11-10)

1. All four kinds in the first version: intervals, chord qualities, scales and modes, scale degrees.
2. The `drill_kind` values: `Intervals by ear`, `Chords by ear`, `Scales by ear`, `Degrees by ear`.
3. Drill notes are notes of the vault's instrument-agnostic **Music Exercise** type (`type/music/exercise`, suffix ` (Music Exercise)`), not guitar or piano exercises. New notes get `exercise_kind: Ear training` (that type's select property: Ear training, Rhythm, Sight-reading, Theory drill) plus `drill_kind`, `drill_setting` and `drill_stats`, which the vault's Music Exercise type defines.
4. The sound (guitar or piano) is a setup choice, written last in the setting's words (`m2-P8, up, piano`): one note per kind and setting, the sound included. Title: `<Kind>, <setting> (Music Exercise)` through the type's affixes.
5. The type is found as the other drills find theirs: the note model's learning type with kind `exercise` and no subject (`type/music/exercise`). A drill property missing in the type: Save says which one, as today.

## Rules

- **Kinds and settings** (each with its sound, `guitar` or `piano`, piano by default):
    - Intervals by ear: the intervals asked (m2 to P15, two octaves; at least 2) and how they sound: `up`, `down`, `together` (harmonic) (at least 1). Default m2-P8, up. Words: a run of 3 or more adjacent intervals as `m2-P8`, else a list (`m3, M3, P5`), then the ways in that order, then the sound: `m2-P8, up, down, together, piano`.
    - Chords by ear: the qualities (major, minor, diminished, augmented, sus2, sus4, dominant 7th, major 7th, minor 7th, half-diminished, diminished 7th; at least 2) and how they sound: `block`, `arpeggio` (at least 1). Default the four triads, block: `major, minor, diminished, augmented, block, piano`.
    - Scales by ear: the scales and modes (major, natural minor, harmonic minor, melodic minor, major pentatonic, minor pentatonic, blues, dorian, phrygian, lydian, mixolydian, locrian; at least 2), played up and back down. Default major, natural minor, harmonic minor, major pentatonic, minor pentatonic.
    - Degrees by ear: `diatonic` (1 to 7, default) or `chromatic` (also b2, b3, #4, b6, b7); `any key` (default) or `key <note>` (`key Eb`): `diatonic, any key, piano`.
- **Words read back** from `drill_setting` in any order of the parts, any case; a part that doesn't read makes the setting unreadable (the note is no drill note), as for the other kinds.
- **Spots** (the map's keys): an interval with its way (`P5 up`, `m3 together`), a chord quality (`minor`, `dominant 7th`), a scale (`dorian`), a degree (`b3`). The key of a degrees drill is in its setting, not in its spots.
- **Questions**: as every drill: `questions` (20), the weak share (60 %) from the weak spots, never a spot twice in a row when there is another one. Each question picks a new root at random: intervals between C3 and G5 (both notes in it), chords on C3 to B3 in close position, scales from G3 to F#4, degrees in the key's tonic from G3 to F#4 (a random key per question with any key).
- **What sounds**: intervals: two notes, 1 s apart (up, down), or together for 1.5 s. Chords: a block for 2 s, or an arpeggio, a note every 0.45 s. Scales: up an octave and back, a note every 0.35 s. Degrees: the cadence I-IV-V-I in the key (a chord every 0.8 s, with the root below), then the note, after 0.6 s.
- **Answers**: buttons, one for each item asked (the guided set, or the whole setting); for intervals the interval only (the way is in the prompt: "Name the interval you hear (up)"). Right: the button of the item asked.
- **Fast answer** (Sébastien, 2026-11-10): ear training has its own fast time, `drills.earFastSeconds`, 4 s by default (0.5 to 20): naming an interval or a chord is slower than finding a note, and with the shared 2 s a beginner would stay on the first two items. It counts for the weak spots, the streak and the guided unlocks of the four ear training kinds (`rulesFor`); notes by ear and the sight drills keep `drills.fastSeconds`.
- **Guided** (Settings → Metronome, with the other drill settings → Guided ear training, on by default): a drill asks only the first items of its setting in a set order, the most distinct first: intervals P8, M3, P5, m3, P4, M2, M6, m7, m2, m6, M7, TT, then the compound ones in the same order (P15, M10, P12, …); chords major, minor, diminished, augmented, dominant 7th, major 7th, minor 7th, sus4, sus2, half-diminished, diminished 7th; scales major, natural minor, major pentatonic, minor pentatonic, harmonic minor, blues, dorian, mixolydian, lydian, phrygian, melodic minor, locrian; degrees 1, 5, 3, 4, 2, 6, 7, b3, b7, b6, #4, b2. It starts with 2 and adds the next one when every item asked so far is cleared (each of its spots: `clearStreak` right answers in a row, each faster than the fast time). An item once asked stays (the set never shrinks). The score says which item the next drill adds.
- **Play again** (R) plays the sound again; on a degrees drill, **Key** plays the cadence and the note again. **Show me** shows the answer and plays the sound again: it counts as a miss.
- **Answer time** from when the last note starts (the player's `onStart` plus the last note's time), never negative; replays don't reset it.
- Without playback (Settings → Playback), the four kinds don't start, as the other drills by ear.
- `exercise_kind` (`exerciseKind`, Settings → Property names): written at the first Save when the type defines it, with the type's allowed value that reads `Ear training` (any case), or `Ear training` when the type lists none; nothing when its list has no such value.

## Build

1. Domain `src/app/domain/ear-training.ts` (pure, specs first): the settings and their words, the items and spots, the guided set, the sounds (events) per kind, the question.
2. `note-drill.ts`: the four kinds in `DrillKind`, `DrillSetting`, `KIND_LABELS`, `drillDefaults`, `settingWords`, `parseSetting`, `spotsOf`, `questions` (the guided set), `isRight`; question type `hear-name`.
3. Service: the Music Exercise type for the four kinds (`exerciseType`), drill notes found on it, `exercise_kind` on Save; field `exerciseKind`.
4. Settings: `drills.guided` (toggle); property name `exerciseKind`.
5. Modal: the setup per kind (toggle buttons for the items, check boxes for the ways), the question (play, Play again, Key, Show me, answer buttons), the result's next guided item, the heat map of every spot in setting order. A command **Ear training** and a panel menu item **Ear training** open the drill on Intervals by ear.
6. Docs: `docs/usage.md`, `docs/configuration.md`, Business Rules, release notes draft, history.
7. Vault check (main session): the four kinds start, sound, answer, save to a Music Exercise note with `exercise_kind: Ear training`, start again from the note.
