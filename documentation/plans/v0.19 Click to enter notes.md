# v0.19: click to enter notes (#76)

Goal: beginners don't know `x32010`. A click on a fretboard or a keyboard enters the frets or the keys, in Add a learning note, Identify this chord and Voicings of a chord, and the chords that hold the notes show when they make no chord the library knows. Prerequisite for #44.

## Decisions (Sébastien, 2026-11-08, through the main session)

1. Guitar: a fretboard (frets 0-12, the layout's hand and fretboard orientation) under the frets field. A click on a string at a fret sets that string's fret; a second click on the same place mutes the string (`x`); fret 0 is open. The frets text and the fretboard stay in sync both ways. Fingers stay automatic (`fingerVoicing`).
2. Piano: a keyboard (C3-B4, the layout's keyboard orientation); clicks toggle keys; feeds pitches (`C4 E4 G4`).
3. Each click sounds its note through Settings → Playback (the play options), as the drills do; nothing sounds when playback is off.
4. Near matches: when the notes make no chord the library detects, up to 6 chords that hold all of them (2 pitch classes at least), fewest missing notes first, from the common chord families on the 12 roots; each names its missing notes, and selecting one marks them faintly on the drawing. The logic is the plugin's (domain, with specs); the library is unchanged.
5. Keyboard access is the library's pick layer (Tab, arrows, Home, End, Space or Enter); presses on the drawing never reach the editor.

## Decisions added while building

6. One reusable control, `ClickInput` (`src/app/ui/click-input.ts`): the library's `renderFretboard` / `renderKeyboard` with `pick: true` and `attachPick`; the chosen notes are drawn by the plugin as dots (with their note names) in its own group right after the library's targets (`g.mp-click-dots`, no pointer events), never by redrawing the drawing, so the focus stays on the target. Chosen targets get `aria-pressed="true"`.
7. Near-match marks: on a keyboard, every key of a missing note in the range; on a fretboard, the missing notes on the muted strings, from 2 frets below the lowest fretted note to 2 above the highest (frets 0-4 when nothing is fretted). Dashed rings (`g.mp-click-near`).
8. Add a learning note: guitar chord types get the fretboard under the name field (typed frets show on it; a click writes frets into the field, the rest as today: chord detected, fingers worked out). Piano chord types get the keyboard: clicks write the keys into the field (`C4 E4 G4`), which now also takes keys typed that way; the chord is detected from them and the note also gets them as its pitches (when its type has the pitches property). A near match picked writes its symbol into the field.
9. Identify this chord: as before when the note's frets or pitches name a chord. When they name none, or the note has none, a modal opens on the note's notes: click to change them; it shows the readings (pick one) or the near matches; "Name the chord" writes the symbol, root, quality and alias as before, and the frets (guitar) or pitches (piano) clicked, when the type has those properties and they changed.
10. Voicings of a chord (no chord given): a fretboard under the Chord field; the chord its frets make fills the field; near matches fill it when picked.

## Specs to write first

- Domain `click-input.ts`: toggling a fret and a key, pitches of frets, reading and writing key lists, near matches (order, limit, spelling, at least 2 notes), near-match marks on a fretboard.
- UI: `ClickInput` in happy-dom: NOT written. The plugin has no DOM library in its specs (UI specs use fakes), and adding happy-dom changes package.json and bun.lock beyond the library pin (the catalog needs a v1 lockfile; a regeneration risks v2). The control's logic is in the domain instead (`afterPick`, `chosen`, `nearTargets`, `readValue`, `valueOfText`, `textOfValue`, `typedKeysOf`, `spellKeys`, `namedNoteChanges`, `clickHint`); the UI glue (dots, rings, `aria-pressed`, focus, sound, the three forms) is checked in the vault.

## Review (fresh agent) and outcome

15 findings and 4 suspicions. Fixed (specs first where the logic is pure: `click-input.spec.ts`, "review findings", 5 specs, each failing on its assertions against stubs first):

1. High: one piano token such as G7, C7, E9 read as a key, so Add a learning note couldn't make G7 and the near match C7 wrote a key: one token is a chord first (`typedKeysOf`).
2. Near matches listed the same notes more than once (Caug, Eaug, Abaug; Dm7 and F6): one per set of notes, the first in the order.
3. Identify wrote new frets or pitches but left the old fingers, position and piano fingering: `namedNoteChanges` writes fingers (`fingerVoicing`) and position (`baseFretOf`, when the note has one), and removes the piano fingering; only the type's properties.
4. Vertical drawings too small (the preview's 180px cap): not capped for the click drawings (CSS).
5. Flats became sharps: typed keys keep their spelling; clicked keys are spelled by their chord, the root on the simpler side (Db F Ab).
6. Identify turned a list of pitches into a string: the shape is kept.
7. Identify opened a six-string fretboard for any instrument: guitar and piano only; others keep the message.
8. Identify could close without writing: the write throws, the modal stays open and says why.
9. Wrong hint with 3 notes or more and no near match: `clickHint` says no common chord holds them.
10. Picking a near match lost the focus: it goes to the chord field (Add a learning note, Voicings of a chord).
11. Voicings kept a chord the frets no longer made: the field follows the frets (empty when they make none).
12. Add a learning note's piano preview ignored typed keys: the preview block gets `pitches:`.
13. Unchosen targets had no `aria-pressed`: `false` on every target, `true` on the chosen ones.
14. The guide said Identify picks a near match like the others: it doesn't, on purpose (naming a note with a chord its notes don't make would contradict the frets it keeps); the guide says so.
15. Specs: the pure parts are covered (above); the UI stays a vault check (no DOM library).

Suspicions: 16 (play options read when the drawing opens, not at each click) kept and documented: `attachPick` takes the options once, and a window rarely outlives a settings change. 17 (removing a note still sounds it) kept, documented. 18 (notes outside the drawing's range) documented: they stay in the field without a dot. 19 (Voicings of this chord on a note with frets but no symbol) not applicable: that command needs a chord symbol, and Voicings of a chord has no note.
