# Rules

How the plugin decides what to practice. These rules are shared by every instrument and every kind of item.

## Mastery levels

Only the number matters (10 to 60); each note type can name its levels its own way. Reaching 20 and 60 stamps the start and mastery dates.

What each level means, per kind:

- **Chord**: 20 shape known; 30 every string rings; 40 switches in time at the target BPM; 60 from memory with any transition.
- **Arrangement or piece**: 20 sections practiced separately; 30 full run-through slowly; 40 full song at tempo with small mistakes; 60 from memory, at tempo, in context.
- **Scale, technique, exercise**: 20 pattern known; 30 clean at a slow BPM; 40 clean at the target BPM; 60 from memory in any position.

## Spaced review

- Reaching 30 starts reviews every 3 days.
- **Good** doubles the interval (up to 90 days), **shaky** keeps it, **failed** resets it to 3 days and drops a mastered item to Rusty.
- An item is **due soon** a few days before its interval ends, and **overdue** after. Mastered items keep being reviewed.

## Practice score

Higher means practice sooner: priority, how long since you last practiced (relative to the review interval), the gap to your target BPM, how many items it unlocks, and a bonus for Rusty items.

## Practice plan

A session of N minutes is split into warm-up (15%), focus (50%), review (20%) and fun (15%), with up to three items per block and at least five minutes each. Empty blocks give their time to focus.

## Ready to learn

An item you want to learn is ready when every prerequisite is at least at "Can play slowly".
