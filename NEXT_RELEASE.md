Every block now has a full screen mode, and tabs and scores make better use of the screen. From music-notation 0.21.0.

#### Full screen on every block

- **Full screen**: a new button at the end of the bar under each block, after **Copy as image**. Chord diagrams, keyboards, fretboards, the circle of fifths, strumming and picking patterns, progressions and chord charts get it; tabs and scores had it since 0.23.0.
- The block grows to the largest size that fits your screen, in width and in height, centered. The bar stays at the bottom, at its usual size. A block too long to fit at any size, such as a long chord chart, is shown up to 1.5 times larger and scrolls.
- The space bar plays and pauses, wherever the focus is (in a text field, Space still types a space).
- More room on the sides, and rows of diagrams are centered.
- Escape, or the button again, takes you back to the note, right where you were.

#### Tabs and scores

- In full screen, the last line is stretched to the full width. A short score is drawn larger, step by step, as long as it still fits, so a one-bar score fills the width. A long score keeps its size.

#### Bars

- The sliders are a little narrower, so a bar fits on one line more often.
